Orchade — Plot Planner & Agronomic Simulation Engine

Orchade is an interactive planning game and agronomic simulation for homesteads, orchards, and productive plots.

Design plot zones, place researched crops, inspect individual plants, and explore water ranges, nutrient drivers, growth conditions, and neighboring plant synergies.

What you can do in Orchade

  • Plan and move planting zones on a plot.
  • Assign crops and inspect plant-level information.
  • Explore water, nutrient, growth, and placement interactions.
  • Experiment with spatial decisions and observe how the simulated plot responds.
